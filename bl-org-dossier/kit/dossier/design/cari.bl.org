* In one breath
#+begin_lede
The Consumer Aesthetics Research Institute keeps a visual lexicon the way a
field station keeps specimens: a white page, a black disc, one gray rule, a
lowercase index, and a gallery you can open. Everything is a record with a
name, a summary, a timeline and a set of examples. This module turns that
catalog voice into a dossier language. It is also its own specimen page.
#+end_lede

#+begin_stats
1 | black disc | the mark is a filled circle and a lowercase word, nothing else
4 | greys do the work | #ffffff paper · #fdfdfd card · #e7e7e7 index bar · #cdcdcd rule
1 | green action | #51ae39, reserved for the one button that moves the work
2 | registers | white archive by day; a black archive at night
#+end_stats
