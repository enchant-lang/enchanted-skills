* In one breath
#+begin_lede
CARI treats every aesthetic as a *specimen*: a white sheet, a lowercase name,
an italic namesake, a gray index bar you open, and a square gallery you can
walk. The stage treats every brand fact the same way. The institute's frame is
neutral and almost colourless, so that the *specimen* — here, Knowledger — can
carry its own colour, and so that the *absence* of a specimen is as visible as
its presence.
#+end_lede

The stage is presented on a wall to a room of thirty people who did not write
the notebook. They should understand the shape of the brand before they read
a sentence: who matters, where doubt peaks, where the map stops.

* What we take from CARI, and why

| CARI habit | Seen on cari.institute | What it does on the stage |
|---+---+---|
| The black disc and a lowercase word | the logo, the menu | The stage's mark, and the "stated fact" glyph: a filled disc means *someone said this*. |
| White paper, four greys | =#ffffff=, =#fdfdfd=, =#e7e7e7=, =#cdcdcd= | The archive frame. Greys carry structure, never meaning. |
| Thin 1 px rules, no shadow | borders everywhere, =box-shadow= nowhere | Every grouping is a rule or a gray bar. Depth only exists inside the 3D scene. |
| The gray index bar (=details summary=) | aesthetic detail sections | A slide's kicker and every category label is an index bar. Click opens the specimen sheet. |
| Square specimen tiles that dim on hover | =.aesthetic-gallery-block=, 1:1, opacity 66 % | The overview (press =o=) is a gallery of slides; persona and asset tiles are squares. |
| Slug + italic namesake | aesthetic header: example · name · /namesake/ | Each slide names its object and, in italics, its source (/Copy Notebook, step 5/). |
| 75 % / 25 % header with a ruled sidebar | =.aesthetic-header= | The specimen sheet slides in as the 25 % column, ruled on its left. |
| One green action | =#51ae39= | Reserved for *what the brand leaves them feeling* — the target, the one thing we steer toward. |
| Epilogue, then mono for facts | Epilogue everywhere | Epilogue at display sizes (one word per slide can be 120 px); IBM Plex Mono for ids, counts and provenance. |

What we do *not* take: the site's density of text, its menus, its jQuery
gallery machinery and its gallery images. The stage keeps the voice, not the
furniture.

* The visual grammar

Every encoding below is a promise: the same mark means the same thing on
every slide, in 2D and in 3D. A reader who learns it on slide two can read
slide nine without a legend.

** Certainty — the most important axis
| Mark | Means | Example |
|---+---+---|
| ● filled ink disc / solid line | *stated* in the source | Karim's quote, the seven steps |
| ◌ dashed outline / dashed line | *inferred* or *assumed* by us | fear → pillar links, the doubt curve, persona placements |
| ○ hollow ring, hatched fill | *expected but missing* — a gap | step 1 has no persona question; no one-page summary exists |
| faint ghost (12 % ink) | *beyond the map* — the framework has a slot the notebook never reached | the meeting, pilot, onboarding, adoption, advocacy |

The gap is never a red label on top of content. It is a *place* that is drawn
and left empty, so the eye finds the hole the way it finds a missing tooth.

** Colour
| Colour | Role | Rule |
|---+---+---|
| Ink =#000= on paper =#fff= | the archive, all text | never decorative |
| Greys =#fdfdfd · #e7e7e7 · #cdcdcd= | structure: cards, bars, rules | never a meaning |
| Green =#51ae39= | the brand's target feeling, the next action | at most one green idea per slide |
| Red =#990000= | a blocking gap: a stall point, a missing asset on a step where the deal can die | only where losing the deal is at stake |
| Four persona hues (copper, slate, violet, teal) | which person | always paired with the person's initial; never the only cue |
| Knowledger copper =#a8452b= | the specimen's own voice: the two words, the French pitch lines | appears only when Knowledger itself speaks |

The institute is colourless; the specimen keeps its colour. That separation
lets the room tell *our analysis* from *the brand talking* at a glance.

** Size, position, motion
- *Size is weight.* A pillar's disc grows with the worries it answers; a
  persona grows with their weight in the decision. Numbers sit beside the
  shape, in mono, so the size can be checked.
- *Height is confidence.* On the journey, up is warmth, down is doubt. Fog in
  the 3D scene is the doubt itself: thick at first sight, thin by the inbox.
- *Left to right is time.* Always. Phases beyond the booked meeting continue
  the axis, drawn as ghosts, so the room sees how much road is left.
- *Motion means arrival.* A person's lane enters the journey at the step where
  they join; nothing moves without a reason. Under reduced motion every scene
  holds its final frame.

** Words
- At rest a slide shows at most a dozen words: a kicker bar, one display word
  or phrase, and labels. Everything else lives in the specimen sheet, one
  click away.
- Lowercase for labels and bars (CARI); sentence case for the customer's own
  words, which always appear in quotation marks and in the persona's hue.
- French lines stay French and stay in italics; the stage does not translate
  the brand's voice.

* Slides as specimens

Each slide is one specimen of the brand, named in the deck's =.bl.org=
heading, with a =:LAYOUT:= that picks one of a small set of figures:

| Layout | Figure | Reads as |
|---+---+---|
| =title= | 3D: the black disc with the five pillars in orbit | "one thing, held by five" |
| =words= | the two expressions, split, copper on paper | "the possessive is the idea" |
| =fears= | three bodies: one shedding, one leaking, one fading to grey; dashed links to pillars | "what they are afraid of, and what answers it" |
| =pillars= | five glyphs sized by the worries they answer | "where the weight of the message sits" |
| =people= | a power × fluency field; four discs, their quotes in orbit | "who decides, who already uses AI" |
| =journey= | 3D ribbon over seven steps, fog = doubt, persona lanes entering, ghost road beyond | the emotion journey |
| =presence= | persona × step matrix; hatched where absent | "who is in the room, when" |
| =worries= | persona → worry → pillar flows, laid on the steps | "every ad, and where it lands" |
| =framework= | the full CX grid, dimensions × phases, filled vs hollow | "how much of the map we have" |
| =process= | four tracks over time: done, now, next, open | "where the brand work stands" |
| =gaps= | the computed holes, ranked, each a hollow ring that opens | "what to do on Monday" |

Gaps are *computed* from the datom model by queries in the kit, not written in
the deck. Change the EDN, and the gap slide changes.

* Checks the prototype must pass
- Every slide reads on a 390 px phone and on a 16:9 wall; no horizontal scroll.
- Both themes: the white archive by day, the black archive at night
  (=dossier.design.cari= dark tokens), toggled with =t=.
- Keyboard: =← →= / space move, =o= overview, =t= theme, =esc= closes the sheet.
- No slide relies on colour alone: hue is always paired with an initial,
  certainty with a line style, gaps with a hatch.
- The scene never invents a figure: every size and position comes from an
  entity attribute that the specimen sheet shows.
